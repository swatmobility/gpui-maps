# Support

gpui-maps and its format crates are free and open source under
MIT OR Apache-2.0, for everyone: individuals, small teams and companies of
any size. Using them in a commercial product needs no permission and no
payment.

## Community support (free)

- **Questions and bugs:** open an issue on
  [GitHub](https://github.com/swatmobility/gpui-maps/issues). Include the
  crate version, your OS and GPU, and the smallest code or camera that
  shows the problem.
- **Security problems:** do not open a public issue; see
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- Community support is best effort and covers the latest release.

## Commercial support

For teams that ship gpui-maps in a product and need commitments that an
open-source licence does not give, SWAT Mobility offers:

- **Support with response times:** a private channel to the engineers who
  build the engine, agreed response times, and help with upgrades.
- **Long-term support releases:** a version kept on security and bug fixes
  without API changes, so you can stay on one GPUI release while the
  ecosystem moves.
- **Custom work:** features built to your schedule and released in the
  open crate, such as a platform port, a data format or a performance
  target.
- **A commercial agreement** for your procurement and legal teams.

Talk to us: [swatmobility.com/contact-us](https://www.swatmobility.com/contact-us).
